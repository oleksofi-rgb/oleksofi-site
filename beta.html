Help Test Trader Assistant

We are preparing Trader Assistant for Android testing and inviting people to express interest in becoming beta testers.

What is Trader Assistant?

Trader Assistant helps you monitor market quotes, choose instruments, set upper and lower price levels, and receive price alerts. It does not place trades or provide investment advice.

How to apply

Email oleksofi@gmail.com with the subject Trader Assistant Beta Tester Application. Please mention your Android version and the language you would like to use. Do not send passwords, payment information or identity documents.

Apply by Email →

What happens next?

We'll review expressions of interest and contact selected testers with instructions when a test build is ready. Sending an email does not guarantee a place in the testing group.

Is this a trading service?

No. Trader Assistant is a market monitoring and notification tool. Quotes and alerts may be delayed or unavailable during connection interruptions.

Your email application will be used to review and respond to your request. Please see our privacy information for details. Before applying, please review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. These documents are being finalized for public launch.
