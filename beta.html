Help test Trader Assistant

We are preparing Trader Assistant for Android testing and inviting interested people to apply.

What is Trader Assistant?

Trader Assistant helps you monitor market prices, choose instruments, set upper and lower price levels and receive alerts. It does not place trades or provide investment advice.

How to apply

Email oleksofi@gmail.com with the subject Trader Assistant Beta Tester Application. Include your Android version and preferred language. Do not send passwords, payment information or identity documents.

Apply by email

What happens next?

We will review applications and contact selected testers when a test build is ready. Sending an email does not guarantee a place.

Important information

Quotes and alerts may be delayed during connection interruptions. Your application email is used to review and respond to your request. Please read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. For privacy questions or questions about these terms, contact OlekSofi by email.
