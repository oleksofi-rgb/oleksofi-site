Frequently Asked Questions

What does Trader Assistant do?

It displays available market prices and alerts you when your selected price levels are reached. It does not place trades.

Why aren't alerts arriving?

If alerts are not arriving, check your internet connection, notification permissions, background operation settings and battery-saving restrictions. Make sure monitoring is enabled for the relevant price level. If the problem persists, contact OlekSofi Support.

How does the trial work?

New users receive a seven-day free trial to explore Trader Assistant. After the trial ends, continued price monitoring and alerts require a monthly or annual subscription through Google Play. Current subscription prices are displayed before purchase confirmation. Users choose their preferred plan. Subscription purchases will be available once Google Play Billing is enabled.

How can I request account or data deletion?

Email oleksofi@gmail.com with the subject “Account or Data Deletion”. Do not send passwords or payment card details. We will verify your request and explain the next steps. If you have an active subscription, cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting an account or uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel a subscription.

How can I manage my subscription?

You can manage your Trader Assistant subscription in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling a subscription does not automatically delete your account. To request account or data deletion, email oleksofi@gmail.com.
