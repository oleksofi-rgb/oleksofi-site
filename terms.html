Terms of Use

Purpose

Trader Assistant is an informational tool for monitoring market quotes and setting price alerts.

OlekSofi aims to provide accurate, timely and reliable market data. However, temporary delays or interruptions may occur due to financial market conditions, internet connectivity and external data providers.

Trader Assistant does not provide investment recommendations, execute trades on users' behalf or guarantee financial outcomes.

User Responsibility

Users make their own trading and investment decisions, assess the associated risks and are responsible for their financial decisions.

Trader Assistant provides informational tools for market monitoring and price alerts to help users receive relevant information in a timely manner.

Before placing trades, users are encouraged to verify current market conditions with their broker.

Free Trial and Subscriptions

Trader Assistant offers new users a seven-day free trial to explore the app's features.

After the trial ends, users may continue using paid features by purchasing a monthly or annual subscription.

Paid subscriptions are purchased through Google Play. Subscriptions renew automatically for the selected period until cancelled.

Current prices and payment terms are displayed in Google Play before purchase confirmation.

Users can cancel automatic renewal at any time in Google Play subscription settings. After cancellation, paid access continues until the end of the current billing period.
