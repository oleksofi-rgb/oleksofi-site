Privacy Policy

OlekSofi · Trader Assistant

Draft document. This page is a draft prepared for publication. Before launch, actual data flows, server logs, backups, future accounts and payment integrations must be verified and the final version approved.

1. Who We Are

Trader Assistant is an application by OlekSofi. For privacy inquiries, contact oleksofi@gmail.com.

2. What the App Does

The app displays available market instruments and quotes, lets users select instruments, set price levels and receive local alerts.

3. Data and Settings

The current test version stores selected instruments, alert levels, language and sound settings, and other preferences on the device. The app connects to the Market Core server component to obtain market data. The technical information that may be recorded in server logs is still being reviewed.

4. Device Permissions

To deliver alerts, the app may request permission for notifications, vibration, display over other apps and background operation. Users can manage these permissions in Android settings.

5. Backups

Depending on system settings, Android may include some local app settings in backups or transfers to another device. Certain technical settings and credentials are excluded from this mechanism.

6. Accounts, Payments and Third-Party Services

Accounts and Free / Pro subscriptions are still in development. Before launch, this policy will be updated with information about registration, payment processing, service providers, retention periods and user rights.

7. Beta Testing Enquiries

People interested in testing Trader Assistant may voluntarily email oleksofi@gmail.com. The email address, message, Android version and preferred language supplied in that message are used to review and respond to the enquiry and, if selected, to provide testing instructions. Applications are handled by email; this website does not currently provide an automated registration form. Please do not send passwords, identity documents or payment details. Email retention and any additional services used to process enquiries will be reviewed before the public recruitment launch.

8. User Requests

For questions about data, corrections or deletion requests, contact oleksofi@gmail.com. The ability to delete local data also depends on Android and app functionality.

9. Updates

This document may change as the product evolves. A final effective date will be provided before public launch.
