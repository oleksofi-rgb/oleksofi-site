Privacy Policy

OlekSofi · Trader Assistant

1. Who We Are

Trader Assistant is an application by OlekSofi. For privacy inquiries, contact oleksofi@gmail.com.

2. What the App Does

The app displays available market instruments and quotes, lets users select instruments, set price levels and receive local alerts.

3. Data and Settings

The current test version stores selected instruments, alert levels, language and sound settings, and other preferences on the device. The app connects to the Market Core server component to obtain market data. Account and service data practices are described below. Technical logs may be used to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service; the exact data collected and retention periods must be confirmed before public release.

4. Device Permissions

To deliver alerts, the app may request permission for notifications, vibration, display over other apps and background operation. Users can manage these permissions in Android settings.

5. Backups

Depending on system settings, Android may include some local app settings in backups or transfers to another device. Certain technical settings and credentials are excluded from this mechanism.

6. Accounts, Payments and Third-Party Services

Trader Assistant user accounts and access permissions are managed through OlekSofi's Market Core server infrastructure.

Subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. OlekSofi does not collect or store full payment card details.

Account information is used to provide access to the service, manage subscriptions and respond to support requests. OlekSofi does not sell users' personal data.

Market price information is obtained from external market data services. These services are separate from the processing of user account and payment information.

Users may request deletion of their accounts and associated personal data, subject to applicable legal and security retention requirements.

7. User Requests

For questions about data, corrections or deletion requests, contact oleksofi@gmail.com. The ability to delete local data also depends on Android and app functionality.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated as the product evolves. Contact oleksofi@gmail.com for the latest privacy information.
